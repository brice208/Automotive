About R&B

Craftsmanship
You Can Trust

R&B Automotive Performance works exclusively with classic cars, hot rods, and American muscle vehicles across McDonough and surrounding communities. When you own a vintage ride, you need a shop that understands the mechanical nuances — not one that approaches every job like a modern vehicle.

Locally owned, American owned, and veteran owned — our operation is built on quality workmanship and doing the job right rather than quickly. Every project receives the same level of care, from a routine tune-up to a full performance overhaul.

Our Services

From diagnostics to full custom builds — everything your classic deserves.

01
Electrical Repair
Experienced troubleshooting and repairs that address the unique wiring challenges in vintage vehicles. We trace problems to their source and restore reliable function.

02
Performance Upgrades
Enhancements designed to improve power, handling, and reliability while respecting original character. Cam swaps, heads, intakes, forced induction — we do it all.

Brake Service
Complete brake system work that ensures stopping power meets modern safety expectations. Pads, rotors, calipers, drums, lines, and performance upgrades.

Custom Exhaust

Classic Car Maintenance
