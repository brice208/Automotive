About R&B

Built on Craft
Driven by Pride

R&B Automotive Performance has been the go-to destination in Henry County for drivers who demand the best. Independently owned and operated, we specialize in classic car repair, hot rod builds, performance upgrades, and custom exhaust fabrication — keeping your classics running like new.

With more than 35 years in the industry, our master technicians bring unmatched expertise to every build. We take great pride in the quality of our workmanship, and that reputation shows in every vehicle that rolls out of our shop.

What We Do

Our Services

From a simple tune-up to a full frame-off restoration, R&B handles it all with the same uncompromising attention to detail.

01
Classic Car Repair
We specialize in keeping your classics and antiques running flawlessly. From carburetor rebuilds to full mechanical restorations, no vintage vehicle is beyond our expertise.
Learn More
02
Performance Upgrades
Unlock the full potential of your engine. We handle cam swaps, head porting, intake manifolds, superchargers, and everything in between to make your ride faster and more reliable.
Learn More
03
Custom Exhaust
Hand-bent, custom-fabricated exhaust systems designed for your exact build. From tucked hot rod pipes to high-flow performance systems — we make it sound as good as it runs.
Learn More
04
Engine Rebuilds
Full engine rebuilds to factory spec or beyond. We machine, balance, and assemble engines with precision — whether it's a numbers-matching restoration or a fire-breathing street monster.
Learn More
05
Brake Service
Complete brake system service including pads, rotors, calipers, drums, and hydraulic lines. We also perform performance brake upgrades for track-day and high-demand applications.
Learn More
06
Electrical & Diagnostics
From vintage wiring harness upgrades to modern diagnostic scans, our team tackles everything from a blown fuse to a full electrical overhaul — done right the first time.
